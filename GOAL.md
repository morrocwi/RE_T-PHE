# GOAL — what the RE_T-PHE research programme is trying to achieve

## Outcome sought

Premarital health education that is **recognised, governed and prospectively tested as primary
prevention infrastructure** for intimate-partner harm and its health consequences — entering before
a family becomes a site of clinical rescue.

## How we will know (outcome-level, not yet measured)

| # | Outcome | Evidence that would show it |
|---|---|---|
| G-1 | T-PHE's programme theory is stated in a form that can be tested and can fail. | The failure criterion in the abstract (C-10 in `CLAIMS.md`) is operationalised: each named outcome domain has a defined instrument. |
| G-2 | The single mechanism (participant control over proceed / delay / refuse, and over help-seeking) is measurable. | A validated or adapted instrument for decisional control exists and has been piloted. |
| G-3 | The safeguarding rule (stop joint work on suspected or disclosed coercive control) is implemented with fidelity. | A process measure is defined and audited. |
| G-4 | A prospective evaluation design exists and is ethically approved. | Protocol, ethics approval, pre-registration. |
| G-5 | The programme theory is presented and critically appraised by the field. | TIMA/FIMA 2026 presentation delivered; appraisal recorded in `docs/reports/`. |

## What is explicitly out of scope for this repository

- Storing or analysing participant-level data.
- Providing guidance to individual couples, educators or clinicians.
- Claiming effectiveness before a prospective evaluation has been run.

## Current position (2026-09-02)

Abstract accepted for TIMA/FIMA Scientific Convention 2026 and filed here as the document of
record. G-1 is partially met (failure criterion stated, instruments not yet defined). G-2 to G-5 are
open.
