## 1. Legal identity

- **ARAYA Nikah Social Enterprise Co., Ltd.**, Bangkok, Thailand. *Registered.*
- Incorporated 26 August 2022 under the former name ARAYA Nikah Planner & Consult Co., Ltd.;
  certified as a social enterprise on 5 November 2024 under Thailand's Social Enterprise Promotion
  Act B.E. 2562 (2019). *Organisation-attested, cross-checked against the organisation's legal-entity
  register; verify with the Department of Business Development and the Office of Social Enterprise
  Promotion before citing in a manuscript.*
- The social-enterprise form binds the company to a declared social objective, limits profit
  distribution, and imposes reporting duties. Its relevance to T-PHE is discussed in the private
  working notes and, if retained, in the manuscript's Discussion; it is not a claim of effectiveness.

## 2. Mission and protected groups

*Organisation-attested (governance core, 2026).*

- Purpose: to create a **safe space, physically, psychologically and economically**, for people who
  differ in religion, gender, ethnicity, language and background, on the understanding that no
  person or institution holds complete knowledge and that people depend on one another in
  distinct roles under human responsibility.
- Declared order of priority: truth and morality; human dignity; epistemic humility; trust
  (amanah) and justice; authority boundaries; physical, psychological and economic safety;
  long-term trust; and, last, money and speed.
- Protected groups named in the governance core: women, children, families, converts to Islam,
  cross-cultural couples, and people facing information asymmetry.
- Safe-space rules: preserve human dignity; do not judge inner worth; do not stereotype; separate
  the person from behaviour and evidence; advise with goodness, compassion and proper manners.

## 3. Authority boundary (what the organisation may and may not do)

*Organisation-attested.* This boundary is the organisational origin of T-PHE's "bounded authority
and referral" safeguard.

- May: explain, prepare, coordinate, consult, design process, and educate within scope.
- Must not: claim state authority; claim fatwa authority; certify on behalf of an external
  authority; guarantee external acceptance; misrepresent status.
- The organisation is not a mosque, a civil marriage registrar, a court, or a religious authority.
  Its course-completion certificate is a private document, not a state, religious, or marriage
  document. No acceptance by any guardian, family, mosque, officiant, embassy, or destination
  country is guaranteed.

## 4. Religious position

*Organisation-attested.* The organisation follows Ahl al-Sunnah wa-l-Jamaʿah across the four Sunni
schools of law (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafiʿi, Hanbali), with an Ashʿari-leaning theological vocabulary,
and operates an internal working discipline described as an eight-node chain: al-Haqq (ultimate
truth) → khalq (createdness: neither humans nor AI are the source of truth) → faqr (recognition of
limits) → qabul (openness to correction) → fahm al-ʿabd (bounded understanding) → taqwa
(God-conscious restraint) → amanah (entrusted responsibility) → hikmah (wisdom), with bayyina
(sufficient evidence: no material claim without evidence) as an operating filter between taqwa and
amanah. Four standing guards cross-cut the chain: justice (ʿadl), mercy and goodness, proper manners,
and safety before speed. The summary in this paragraph is a compression of the organisation's
record, not the record itself. The organisation states explicitly that this is an internal
operating discipline and **not a fatwa**. Questions of religious validity
are referred to qualified scholars and the relevant Islamic committee.

## 6. Problems the organisation says it addresses

*Organisation-attested.* Culture and family conflict; unsafe or secret nikah; coercion or
trafficking risk; legal and documentation complexity; weak Muslim cultural literacy among
partners and families. These are the recurring concerns that the T-PHE synthesis drew on; they
are stated here as the organisation's own framing, not as prevalence findings.

## 7. Services relevant to T-PHE

*Organisation-attested.* Authority-bound nikah services; cross-cultural family consultation; an
Islamic learning safe space; conversion guidance; training and certification readiness; legal and
document coordination. The premarital course exists in a course-only form and in forms combined
with a group nikah ceremony; completion of the course confers no automatic right to a ceremony.

## 8. Theory of change and impact framing

*Organisation-attested (Family of Peace theory of change, dated 13 July 2025).*

- Impact goals map to Sustainable Development Goals 5 (women and children protected from
  trafficking and fraudulent marriage), 8 (responsible family leadership and economic management
  among working-age Muslim men), and 16 (stable, resilient families as the origin of peace).
- Intellectual roots named by the organisation: structural and cultural violence (Galtung) and
  practical peace built through action in real settings (Thai peace-studies tradition).
- The organisation lists its assumptions explicitly, including that comprehensive premarital
  training reduces divorce risk. **T-PHE does not rest on that assumption**; T-PHE's failure
  criterion deliberately excludes marriage continuation as a success measure.
