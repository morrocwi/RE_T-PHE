# 02. Premarital and relationship education: measured outcomes

*Scope:* What relationship and premarital education has and has not measured; meta-analyses and trials.

*Provenance of the text in this table:* the **Finding** and **Relevance annotation** columns are AI-drafted readings of the PubMed abstract, checked by an independent verifier; they are not the authors' own wording. The **Verifier** column shows the verdict and, for FIX entries, the correction that must be applied before citing.

*Search queries run (PubMed E-utilities):*

- `(premarital OR "marriage preparation" OR "marriage and relationship education" OR "marital education") AND (counseling OR education OR program) AND (outcome OR effectiveness)`
- `"systematic review"[pt] AND (premarital OR "marriage preparation" OR "premarital counseling") AND (intimate partner violence OR coercion OR domestic violence) [0 results, reformulated]`
- `systematic review[Title/Abstract] AND (premarital[Title/Abstract] OR marriage preparation[Title/Abstract]) AND (violence[Title/Abstract] OR coercion[Title/Abstract])`
- `"Marriage"[Mesh] AND "Counseling"[Mesh] AND ("Intimate Partner Violence"[Mesh] OR "Domestic Violence"[Mesh])`
- `Hawkins AND "marriage education" AND meta-analysis [0 results, reformulated]`
- `marriage education[Title/Abstract] AND meta-analysis[Title/Abstract]`
- `(faith-based OR religious OR church OR mosque OR clergy) AND ("premarital counseling" OR "marriage preparation" OR "marriage education") AND outcomes`
- `premarital counseling AND help seeking`
- `premarital education randomized controlled trial`
- `"Premarital Examinations"[Mesh] OR "premarital screening" AND consanguinity AND counseling [genetics angle, used only for pool scan, not selected]`

*Limits stated by the searcher:* Search was restricted to PubMed via NCBI E-utilities only, per hard constraints; no WHO/UN/USPSTF/Cochrane full-text database, PsycINFO, or grey literature was searched, so meta-analyses/programmes only indexed in psychology-specific or non-PubMed databases (e.g., some PREP/marriage-education literature lives primarily in PsycINFO) may be under-represented despite PubMed's partial MEDLINE overlap with psychology journals. Search terms focused on relationship/marriage EDUCATION and its outcomes (per assigned angle); the much larger PubMed literature on premarital GENETIC/disease SCREENING (thalassemia, consanguinity, HIV, hepatitis) was deliberately excluded as out of this angle's scope even though many such records appeared in the initial pooled search (124 unique PMIDs across 8 queries) -- that screening literature is presumably covered by a different angle's search. All abstracts were read via efetch before being summarized; no full-text articles were accessed. Several older records (pre-2000) were retained because they represent the historical evidence base the modern MRE meta-analyses (Hawkins et al.) are built on, but their methodology is dated and should be weighted accordingly.
