# 04. Couple-based work and coercive control

*Scope:* When joint work is contraindicated; coercive-control measurement.

*Search queries run (PubMed E-utilities):*

- `couples therapy intimate partner violence meta-analysis`
- `conjoint treatment intimate partner violence exclusion criteria`
- `coercive control assessment measurement instrument`
- `domestic violence screening couples counseling safety`
- `intimate partner violence premarital counseling`
- `conjoint couples treatment domestic violence when appropriate`
- `coercive control intimate partner violence measurement scale`
- `situational couple violence versus coercive controlling violence typology`
- `couples therapy contraindicated domestic violence safety concerns`
- `intimate partner violence screening tool clinical practice`
- `batterer intervention program couples counseling outcomes`

*Limits stated by the searcher:* This search covered 11 PubMed queries via NCBI E-utilities (esearch/esummary/efetch), reading full abstracts for 20 of the highest-relevance records out of ~70 unique PMIDs surfaced; it is not a systematic review and does not claim exhaustive coverage. Several broad terms ('coercive') returned substantial off-topic noise (psychiatric coercion, food-parenting-practice scales) that had to be filtered by title/abstract triage rather than retrieved directly. No non-PubMed-indexed grey literature, non-English-language Thai sources, or unpublished practice guidelines were searched, per the hard constraint to use PubMed-indexed literature and global-level guidance (WHO/UN/USPSTF/Cochrane) only; only one USPSTF-commissioned systematic review (Feltner 2018/JAMA) met the global-guidance tier in this angle. No record found or cited states or implies that T-PHE itself is effective; all findings are about the surrounding literature on couple-work safety, IPV typology, and coercive-control measurement/screening, offered as context for evaluating T-PHE's stop-rule and safeguard design, not as evidence of T-PHE's outcomes.
