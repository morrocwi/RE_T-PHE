# 13. Digital self-assessment and safety decision aids for intimate-partner violence

*Scope:* Web, app and online self-assessment or decision-aid trials used by the person alone; anonymous self-screening; safety of technology-delivered tools.

The **Verifier** column shows the verdict and, for FIX entries, the correction that must be applied before citing.

*Limits stated by the searcher:* Search was restricted to PubMed via NCBI E-utilities with English-language, PICO-style queries centred on adult IPV decision-aid and screening literature; grey literature, non-PubMed-indexed Thai-language sources, and non-IPV framed adolescent/premarital relationship-education self-assessment tools (e.g. general SRH knowledge quizzes without an IPV/safety framing) were not systematically searched. Evidence levels above are as stated in each abstract only; no independent methodological quality appraisal was performed beyond what the source study itself reports. This search does not establish that T-PHE's own Layer 0 self-check would be effective - it positions the design choice within existing evidence on structurally similar tools tested in other populations and settings.
