# 14. Peer online support, moderated communities and digital safe spaces

*Scope:* Online peer support for relationship safety and young people's mental health; moderation; technology-facilitated abuse and surveillance risks.

*Provenance of the text in this table:* the **Finding** and **Relevance annotation** columns are AI-drafted readings of the PubMed abstract, checked by an independent verifier; they are not the authors' own wording. *Limits stated by the searcher:* Restricted to PubMed-indexed records retrievable via NCBI E-utilities esearch/efetch with title/abstract-field queries; grey literature, non-indexed platform-safety reports, and non-English-language studies (e.g., Thai-language journals not in PubMed) were not searched. Seven queries were run and abstracts read for 20 selected records out of 98 unique candidate PMIDs identified; record selection was investigator-judged for angle relevance, not a formal systematic-review screening process, so this is a scoping literature snapshot, not a systematic review. No study directly tests a peer community structurally identical to T-PHE's proposed Layer 3, so all relevance judgments are by analogy to structurally similar online peer-support, IPV-support, or technology-facilitated-abuse literature.
