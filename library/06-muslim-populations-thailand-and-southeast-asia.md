# 06. Muslim populations, Thailand and Southeast Asia

*Scope:* Regional and population-specific evidence; explicitly labelled where not Thai.

*Provenance of the text in this table:* the **Finding** and **Relevance annotation** columns are AI-drafted readings of the PubMed abstract, checked by an independent verifier; they are not the authors' own wording. The **Verifier** column shows the verdict and, for FIX entries, the correction that must be applied before citing.

*Search queries run (PubMed E-utilities):*

- `(intimate partner violence[MeSH] OR IPV) AND (Muslim OR Islam*) AND (Thailand OR Malaysia OR Indonesia OR "Southeast Asia")`
- `(forced marriage OR fraudulent marriage OR early marriage OR child marriage) AND Muslim`
- `(premarital OR pre-marital) AND (education OR course OR counseling OR counselling) AND (Islam* OR Muslim)`
- `help-seeking AND intimate partner violence AND Muslim`
- `One Stop Crisis Center Thailand`
- `religious leader AND domestic violence AND response`
- `unregistered marriage OR religious marriage AND Southeast Asia`
- `child marriage AND (Thailand OR Malaysia OR Indonesia)`
- `kursus pra nikah OR premarital course Malaysia OR Indonesia`
- `Rohingya AND marriage`
- `polygamy AND Southeast Asia`
- `domestic violence Muslim Malaysia`
- `faith based intervention intimate partner violence`
- `marriage counseling program evaluation Muslim couples`

*Limits stated by the searcher:* This search is restricted to PubMed-indexed literature and PubMed's own indexing/abstracting conventions (some records, e.g. non-English-language items with an English abstract, systematic reviews, and older records, vary in abstract completeness); no WebSearch/WebFetch or non-PubMed grey literature was used, so relevant Thai-language, Malay-language, or Indonesian-language non-PubMed-indexed evaluations of premarital courses, OSCC operations, or faith-leader IPV response (which likely exist in national/ministry or NGO reports) are not captured here. Retrieval used English-language esearch queries only; the true PubMed coverage of Thai/Malay/Indonesian Muslim-population premarital and IPV literature may be under-represented by keyword choice. All 23 records were confirmed via efetch abstract text (not esummary title alone) before being described; two records (PMID 36429857, 34527969) initially appeared to lack abstracts due to an XML-parsing artifact from embedded bold-tag markup and were re-verified with full abstract text present.
